What is a Root Canal?

A root canal is a procedure that removes infected or damaged tissue (the pulp) from inside a tooth. The inside of the tooth is then cleaned, disinfected, filled and sealed. It relieves tooth nerve pain, stops the spread of infection, and lets you keep your natural tooth instead of having it pulled.

Signs You May Need a Root Canal

  • Tooth nerve pain:

    A deep, throbbing or sharp ache that lingers, wakes you at night, or gets worse when you bite down.

  • Lingering sensitivity:

    Pain from hot or cold that continues long after the food or drink is gone.

  • Swelling or a gum bump:

    Swollen gums, facial swelling, or a pimple-like bump near the tooth can point to an infected tooth. Call us promptly.

  • Darkened or cracked tooth:

    A tooth that changes color or is deeply cracked or decayed may have nerve damage even if the pain comes and goes.
